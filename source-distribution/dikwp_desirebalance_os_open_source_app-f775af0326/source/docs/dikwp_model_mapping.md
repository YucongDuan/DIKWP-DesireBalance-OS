# DIKWP Mapping

- D: stated desires, budgets, hours, debts, signals and outcomes.
- I: relations between desires, actors, incentives, scarcity and externalities.
- K: economic, psychological, organizational and platform mechanisms.
- W: trade-offs among autonomy, prosperity, fairness, safety and sustainability.
- P: goal, value, constraint, time and feedback contract.
- R: evidence, residuals, reversibility, kill and recovery conditions.
